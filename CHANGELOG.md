# WearGraph Changelog

### Version: 1.0.0 ###
* **New:** Bring your own AI provider key: Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Black Forest Labs FLUX or xAI; choose which provider makes product images, try-on previews and alt text
* **New:** AI fitting room on product pages: photo upload or camera, consent before upload, option switching without re-upload, Add to Cart, "Delete my photos"
* **New:** Try On button placed automatically, as a block, or with the `[weargraph_try_on]` shortcode; preview mode for store managers
* **New:** Product Studio in the product editor: on-model, flat-lay and lifestyle images from your own product photos; alt text suggestions; save to the Media Library, product image or gallery without overwriting originals
* **New:** Catalog readiness scan (no AI, no cost) with reasons, per-product and bulk overrides
* **New:** Dashboard with try-ons, add-to-cart after try-on, images generated, estimated AI cost and failed generations
* **New:** Privacy: session or 24-hour retention, hourly clean-up, WordPress personal-data export and erase, suggested privacy-policy text, delete-all tool
* **New:** Cost control: per-request and daily caps, limits per visit, per connection and per day
